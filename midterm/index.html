Life of a Starfish

Starfish live in oceans around the world. They are known for their beautiful shape and bright colors. These marine animals can regenerate lost arms and live in many ocean environments.

Facts About Starfish

  • Starfish are marine animals.
  • They usually have five arms.
  • They can regenerate lost limbs.
  • They live on the ocean floor.
